# Mr.Brog 41 1

## Description


![Attachments and files/6-983-3p.png\|500](/img/user/Attachments%20and%20files/6-983-3p.png)

A sleek and unusually shaped Mr. Brog pipe with flattened sides and an elegant, almost keel-like profile. The shaping gives the pipe a refined and modern appearance reminiscent of a golf club head, while the asymmetrical stem work adds additional character. The pipe is in exceptionally clean condition with very little visible use, making it one of the standout pieces so far. Although the stem material appears to be plastic rather than acrylic or vulcanite, the pipe still feels highly usable and has strong potential as a future daily smoker

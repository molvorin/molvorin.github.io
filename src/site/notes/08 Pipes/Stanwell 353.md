## Description
![Attachments and files/34-998-3.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/34-998-3.png)

A Stanwell Royal Briar billiard with a saddle stem, carrying a registration number and shape 353. Very good draw and a well-restored chamber. A few minor nicks consistent with being an estate pipe, but the birds-eye and flame grain -- including a rose-like circular grain pattern -- make this a standout. One of the owner's favorites in the batch.

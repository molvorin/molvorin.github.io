## Description

A near-unsmoked B&B 019 fitted with a filter and finished in a practical semi-bent shape. The chamber and filter show little evidence of use, suggesting the pipe may never have been smoked. Although the finishing is somewhat rough and utilitarian, the pipe remains attractive as a reliable working smoker. Its straightforward design and excellent condition make it one of the better B&B examples featured so far.

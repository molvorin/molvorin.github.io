## Description

An elegant, slim no-name pipe and one of the first the owner ever got, smoked a great deal and always enjoyed. The finish is very smooth and soft, with a few light fills, and the stem's airway sits slightly off-centre, suggesting it may be handmade. It is tall with a round shank that flares outward and then keels inward toward the chamber rim, with a fairly small chamber. The exact shape is uncertain, possibly a Rhodesian. There is no real flaw, just some interior cleaning needed, much of which the owner attributes to having smoked it too hot early on. A personal favourite despite being unidentified.

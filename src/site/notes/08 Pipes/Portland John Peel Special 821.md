## Description
![Attachments and files/41-777-1.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/41-777-1.png)

A Portland John Peel Special, made wholly in England, stamped JP on the stem with an R mark at the heel and the number 821. Thin bezel rim around the chamber and a distinctive slanted head. Mouthpiece and chamber both need cleaning but otherwise decent.

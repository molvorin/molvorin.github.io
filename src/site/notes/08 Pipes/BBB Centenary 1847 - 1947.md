## Description

A BBB Centenary pipe produced in England to commemorate the company’s hundred-year anniversary between 1847 and 1947. The pipe presents a refined and elegant silhouette with restrained proportions and classic English styling. Age-related discoloration of the stem and light surface wear are present, but the pipe remains structurally sound and well preserved. The commemorative stamping adds historical significance, making it appealing both as a smoking instrument and as a collectible piece of pipe-making history.

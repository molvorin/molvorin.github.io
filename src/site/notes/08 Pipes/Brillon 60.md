## Description
![Attachments and files/31-734-3.png\|500](/img/user/Attachments%20and%20files/31-734-3.png)

A Swedish system pipe stamped BRILLON 60, of the same interchangeable, Falcon-style design as a dollar pipe, with a wooden bowl and the draft hole at the bottom. The body and mouthpiece are plastic, the latter a bit chewed and slightly melted, soft and easy to bite hard on, and the pipe is light at one end and heavy at the other. There is some cake in the chamber but the rest is decent. The owner admits he liked the idea of it more than the pipe itself, and is unsure how best to restore a soft plastic mouthpiece. The maker behind Brillon still needs researching.

## Description

An Italian pipe stamped Camillo / Pipa Gasparini, shape 207. Heavily rusticated with a flattened shank and fishtail mouthpiece. Notably long with thick chamber walls -- longer than both the Jeandet and the Stanwell in this batch. A unique pipe the owner really likes, though he suspects it may be too large to be universally popular.

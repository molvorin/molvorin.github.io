## Description

A Baronet 3 Star stamped 106, with what appears to be the original B-marked mouthpiece. A semi-bent with a saddle stem and an appealing shape, but badly damaged: the entire rim top is gone and needs rebuilding, and a crack follows the grain down the side all the way through into the chamber. A shame, because the bones are good.

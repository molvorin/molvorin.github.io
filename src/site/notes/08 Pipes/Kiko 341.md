## Description
![Attachments and files/43-577-9.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/43-577-9.png)

A Kiko 341 made in Tanganyika, wrapped in leather with a slightly slanted mouthpiece. An interesting African pipe with nice leatherwork and a good draw, though there's a fair amount of cake inside and it's unclear yet whether the bowl is lined with meerschaum or just built-up cake. The mouthpiece will need some restoration. Recording cut out partway through filming this pipe, so some detail may be missing.

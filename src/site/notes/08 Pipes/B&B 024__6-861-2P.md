# B&B 024

## Description

An unusual B&B filter pipe with a darker, more subdued appearance and an unidentified wood grain that differs from briar. The pipe has a slightly harder stem material with visible tooth wear, but otherwise remains in solid overall condition. Its proportions and shaping are attractive without being particularly flashy, making it feel like a practical everyday smoker rather than a collector’s piece. The pipe stands out mostly because of the mystery surrounding the wood type and construction materials, both of which remain uncertain.

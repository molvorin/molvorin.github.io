## Description

A Lecor-branded pipe distinguished by attractive grain patterns, including birdseye and flame grain, but affected by significant structural issues. Cracks in the shank and a poorly fitting stem require careful restoration before the pipe can return to regular service. Despite these problems, the quality of the briar remains evident and gives the pipe considerable visual appeal. The combination of strong grain and repair challenges makes it an interesting restoration project with the potential for a rewarding outcome.

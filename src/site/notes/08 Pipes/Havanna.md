## Description

A French-made Havana Breuer Extra pipe with a low, broad bowl and a repaired shank reinforced by a metal band. The pipe displays signs of age and use, including scratches, discoloration, and evidence of previous structural repair, yet remains serviceable and intact. Its shallow but wide chamber creates a distinctive smoking profile compared with deeper traditional bowls. The combination of French manufacture, visible restoration history, and unusual proportions gives the pipe a character shaped as much by its use as by its original craftsmanship.

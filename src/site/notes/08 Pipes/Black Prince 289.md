## Description

A Black Prince 289, made in England, with tape around the mortise and very thin walls. The mouthpiece is not original. This is essentially a pipe head rather than a complete pipe — the stem has been separated and the head moved to the pipe head box for future restoration practice. A sad remnant of what was once a recognisable English brand.

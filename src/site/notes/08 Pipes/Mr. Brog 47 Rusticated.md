## Description
![Attachments and files/32-161-3P.png\|500](/img/user/Attachments%20and%20files/32-161-3P.png)

A rusticated Mr. Brog 47 from the Poland batch with attractive greenish staining, let down by a major flaw: a crack that runs all the way through to the bowl. Mr. Brog uses pear wood rather than briar, so the defect may simply be part of the wood. Besides the crack the pipe is decent, and the owner has enjoyed smoking plenty of these 47s, but the crack drops the condition to the bottom of the scale. A sad case that may be worth passing on to someone who could attempt a repair.

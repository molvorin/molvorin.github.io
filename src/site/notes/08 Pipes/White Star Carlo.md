## Description
![Attachments and files/33-886-2.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/33-886-2.png)

A White Star Carlo, believed to be a Danish company though again made in Eastern Europe, once mistaken for an American Lone Star. A straight pipe with a nice finish and a star logo. The transition between shank and stem is not as refined as a handmade pipe and sits at a slight angle, but it smokes well, is easy to clean and remains an almost-daily smoker. The graining is unremarkable with some birch-side, and the buffing is a touch soft, but the owner is fond of it. No stamped numbers.

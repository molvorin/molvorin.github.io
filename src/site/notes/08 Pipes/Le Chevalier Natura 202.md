## Description
![Attachments and files/42-654-1.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/42-654-1.png)

A Le Chevalier Natura 202, part of the Collezione 1988 line. The stamp includes a knight motif, matching the brand name. Thick walls with a rounded rim, and a ring between the mouthpiece and shank that differs slightly in color. Acquired locally from a previous owner who hadn't smoked it in about 20 years. Not in great shape but not bad either; the vulcanite mouthpiece has some tooth wear that should be repairable. Smokes decently, though it isn't a personal favorite.

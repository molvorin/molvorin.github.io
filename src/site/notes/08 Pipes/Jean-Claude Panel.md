## Description
![Attachments and files/34-977-8.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/34-977-8.png)
![Attachments and files/34-977-8+a.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/34-977-8+a.png)

An unnumbered pipe stamped only Jean-Claude, with a shiny finish and an unusual near-freehand panel shape -- square on three sides, rounded toward the fishtail mouthpiece. Semi-bent. Graining is a little dull with only a few flame streaks, and there are some light scratches, but the distinctive cross-section makes it stand out.

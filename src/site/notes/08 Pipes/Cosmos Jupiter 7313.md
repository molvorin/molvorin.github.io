## Description
![Attachments and files/33-886-1.png\|500](/img/user/Attachments%20and%20files/33-886-1.png)

An affordable Cosmos with a galaxy-style logo, believed to be a Danish company though the pipes are made somewhere in Eastern Europe. The stamp reads Jupiter (the finish) and 73 13 (the shape). A semi-bent with a narrow tenon but thick walls around the mortise, so it can take some rough handling, which suits the owner since he does not baby his daily pipes. Smoked a great deal but clean, with no real issues beyond a small fill. One of the owner's favourite everyday smokers, valued for the shape and the way it smokes.

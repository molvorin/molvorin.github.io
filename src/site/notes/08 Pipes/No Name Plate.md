## Description
![Attachments and files/36-333-1DK.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/36-333-1DK.png)

A classic billiard no name with a decorative metal plate that bears no markings. The replacement mouthpiece has a hole and is heavily chewed, the transition between bowl and mouthpiece is poor, and there is heavy caking throughout. A massive bowl that holds some appeal but overall a disappointing pipe.

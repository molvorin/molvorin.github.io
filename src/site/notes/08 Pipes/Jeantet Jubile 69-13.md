## Description
![Attachments and files/34-977-1PR.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/34-977-1PR.png)

A Canadian-shaped estate pipe with a very flattened shank and a flat mouthpiece that tapers inward. Nice rustication and a good color, with a very good draft. The mouthpiece sits a little loose and may need warming to seat better. Chamber is notably deep. A restored piece the owner is very happy with.

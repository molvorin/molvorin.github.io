## Description
![Attachments and files/42-776-5.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/42-776-5.png)

A massive, unsmoked no-name Danish freehand with plateau top, prominent bird's-eye and straight graining, and a high-heel shank design. Clearly handcrafted. The vulcanite mouthpiece has residue at the mortise that needs cleaning, and there's a small filled nut in the wood. Purchased for roughly 250 kroner including shipping. An extremely unique, oversized piece, though impractical to actually smoke given the sheer chamber size.

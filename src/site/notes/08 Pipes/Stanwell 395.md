## Description
![Attachments and files/35-342-1DK.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/35-342-1DK.png)

A cracked Stanwell 395 with faint stamping and a heavily chewed mouthpiece with a hole in the bottom. Despite a crack running almost a third of the diameter and thick caking inside, the tall chamber is appealing and the tenon fits nicely in the mortise. Found unexpectedly in a cheap lot — a ruined pipe that was once something worth smoking.

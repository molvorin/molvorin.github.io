## Description
![Attachments and files/34-987-9.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/34-987-9.png)

A huge billiard stamped Saint-Claude, France 1508, with a Corrida mark and a Butz-Choquin stamp on top, plus BC stamped on the stem. Flattened shank and mouthpiece, nice graining, and a very large chamber -- bigger than both the Chap and the Vauen 8261. A well-executed restoration that closes out the batch.

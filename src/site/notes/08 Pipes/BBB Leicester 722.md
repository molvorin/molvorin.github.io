## Description
![Attachments and files/38-666-5.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/38-666-5.png)

A BBB Leicester, stamp number 722, in what might be a volcano-adjacent shape. There's a crack in the mortise, but the stem still seats well and the crack looks repairable. Heavily packed chamber. A metallic BBB stamp sits on the mouthpiece, and the shank once read "London made" though it's mostly worn away now.

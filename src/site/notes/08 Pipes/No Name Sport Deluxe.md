## Description
![Attachments and files/33-466-5.png\|500](/img/user/Attachments%20and%20files/33-466-5.png)

A chunky briar bought in France and not part of the daily rotation, brought in to round out the video. It is stamped Sport Deluxe along with "Vieille Bruyère" (French for "old briar", a quality/material marking rather than a maker) and carries an S-shaped logo, so it is catalogued as a no-name. It has an unusual shape with flattened areas on the sides, front and back of the bowl, attractive bird's-eye and straight grain, and a stem made of horn or bone. The main problems are a very loose metal tenon (a previous owner wrapped cotton thread around it to tighten the fit) and a draft hole blocked by something that needs clearing, plus a hole in the horn stem that might be filled with clear epoxy. The owner is unsure how best to fix the loose tenon and welcomes suggestions. A nice, chunky pipe he hopes to restore.

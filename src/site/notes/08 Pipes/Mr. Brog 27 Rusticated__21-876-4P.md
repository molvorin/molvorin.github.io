# Mr. Brog 27 Rusticated


## Description
![Attachments and files/21-876-4P.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/21-876-4P.png)

A variation of the Mr. Brog 27 featuring a darker finish and partial rustication.

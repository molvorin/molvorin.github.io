## Description
![Attachments and files/43-766-8.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/43-766-8.png)

A no-name bulldog stamped 'Barry' and 'Real Briar'. Appears handmade, as the mouthpiece only fits one way against the shank. Smooth finish and decent condition overall, just needs a cleaning. A likeable pipe, especially given a fondness for the bulldog shape, though the graining is a bit plain.

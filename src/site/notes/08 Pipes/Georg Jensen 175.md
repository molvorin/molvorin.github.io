## Description
![Attachments and files/41-887-8.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/41-887-8.png)

A Georg Jensen 175, Made in Denmark, with a push-pull style mouthpiece. Fine flame grain on one side and bird's-eye on the other, with the Jensen stamp still visible. A bit sticky and needs mouthpiece cleaning, but a strong pipe to close out the batch on.

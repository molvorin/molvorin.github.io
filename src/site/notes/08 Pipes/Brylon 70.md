## Description
![Attachments and files/40-624-4.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/40-624-4.png)

A Swedish Brylon 70 - briar and nylon combined, Falcon-style with a flexible stem and screw-on head. Filthy with a packed mouthpiece and a gap between shank and mouthpiece. Draw hole sits at the bottom, so it needs a filter.

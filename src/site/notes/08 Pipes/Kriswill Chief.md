## Description
![Attachments and files/39-765-6.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/39-765-6.png)

A Kriswill Chief, Made in Denmark, with a huge chamber and a comfortable saddle stem. The stamped number is too faint to read reliably. Kriswill is a Danish maker that originally traded under a different name before a naming dispute with Stanwell forced a rebrand. Large pipe with a nice grain, closes out a very strong batch for the price paid.

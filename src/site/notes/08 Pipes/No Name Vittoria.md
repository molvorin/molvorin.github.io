## Description
![Attachments and files/42-565-3.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/42-565-3.png)

A no-name pipe with 'Vittoria' stamped on it, likely Italian in origin. Some charring around the rim and chew marks on the mouthpiece, but a nice flattened rim. Will need a buffing, but has decent bones and could be a good project for learning restoration.

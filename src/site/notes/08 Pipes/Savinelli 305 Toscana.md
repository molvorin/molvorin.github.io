## Description
![Attachments and files/42-678-2.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/42-678-2.png)

A Savinelli 305 Toscana with excellent straight graining and a distinctive triangular shank and mouthpiece, suggesting hand-adjusted or handcrafted finishing. Decent draw and a genuinely enjoyable smoke. The rim is a touch too rounded, keeping it just short of top marks, but overall one of the standout pipes of this batch.

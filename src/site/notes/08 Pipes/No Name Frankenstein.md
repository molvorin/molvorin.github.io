## Description
![Attachments and files/43-444-3.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/43-444-3.png)

A two-piece pipe where the bowl and mouthpiece were found separately and had to be fitted together, hence the nickname. Possibly a Brebbia based on a faint partial stamp, though it's too worn to confirm with certainty. There's a chip missing from the mortise, but the mouthpiece still fits reasonably well despite not obviously being the original pairing.

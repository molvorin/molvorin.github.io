# No Name French pipe

## Description


![Attachments and files/10-876-5D.png\|500](/img/user/Attachments%20and%20files/10-876-5D.png)
An unusual pipe believed to be of French origin, distinguished by a bakelite construction rarely encountered in traditional briar pipes. The pipe features a tall bowl, delicate proportions, and an especially distinctive stem design that connects to the bottom of the bowl, and contributes significantly to its visual appeal. Although unmarked, the pipe appears well preserved and requires little more than routine cleaning. Its uncommon construction and unusual visual presentation give it a novelty value that extends beyond ordinary smoking pipes while remaining fully functional as a smoker

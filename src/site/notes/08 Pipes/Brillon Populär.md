## Description
![Attachments and files/31-645-4.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/31-645-4.png)

A Brillon Populär set, the first multi-part set featured in the series, rated as a whole. It comprises a plastic body with a light decorative band, several interchangeable wooden chamber heads (mostly briar) in different sizes, and an extra, nicer mouthpiece. The chambers carry varying amounts of cake, one quite thick, and the set will need a heavy clean, though nothing critical. The appeal lies in the functionality: rather than carrying multiple pipes or resting a bowl for a day, you can simply swap heads on the plastic base. Not a pipe that blew the owner away, but the system idea is genuinely interesting.

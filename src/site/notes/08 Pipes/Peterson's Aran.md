## Description
![Attachments and files/43-656-4.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/43-656-4.png)

A very pleasant surprise pull from the box: a Peterson's Aran, made in the Republic of Ireland, stamped with the number 25 and a 'P' on the original mouthpiece. There's a small gap between the shank and stem that may need adjusting, and the chamber and rim need cleaning, but the draw is decent and it's sturdy enough to see real use. Robust shank and stem with a comparatively small bowl.

## Description

A Ben Hur pipe — B-E-N H-U-R — with a very rough rustication that looks more like a pattern than a traditional finish. The long tenon fits nicely in the mortise and the mouthpiece appears to be vulcanite with some scratches but nothing critical. An unfamiliar brand with a unique look that does not quite match personal taste but may interest others for its distinctiveness.

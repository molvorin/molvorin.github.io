## Description

A Stanwell found in its original box marked 882 on the outside. The pipe itself is stamped 53 and Made in Denmark with an S-stamped original mouthpiece. The shape — a short billiard with a saddle stem — is a personal favourite. Likely a Stanwell Royal Prince 53, a confirmed Stanwell line. Heavy caking in the chamber and a significant gap in the mouthpiece bit that has been filed thin. Will need restoration work but worth it.

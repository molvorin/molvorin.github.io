## Description
![Attachments and files/37-342-5DK.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/37-342-5DK.png)

A no name pipe with bird's eye grain and a distinctive metal ring on the mouthpiece that resembles a finger ring. Someone previously attempted a repair with gorilla tape over a hole in the shank — the tape has since done more damage than repair, leaving a sticky residue and surface damage. The rim is uneven and there is a gap at the shank. Has potential if the damage can be addressed.

## Description
![Attachments and files/34-778-6.png\|500](/img/user/Backroom/Attachments%20and%20files/34-778-6.png)

A half-bent billiard with a saddle stem and brass ring. The stummel stamp has been sanded or filed off, but the mouthpiece still confirms Georg Jensen. A few nicks too deep to fully restore and a slightly loose stem, but otherwise a typical, solid example of the brand -- one the owner expects Danish viewers especially will respond well to.

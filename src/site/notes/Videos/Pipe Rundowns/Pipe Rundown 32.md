## Description

This episode of Pipe Rundown works through five more estate pipes from the collection, a varied lot ranging from a French-bought Mokin with a meerschaum lining to a couple of well-worn Mr. Brog pipes from the Poland batch. Along the way there is a genuine mystery pipe stamped MRS·BRO 64 in real briar with a repaired stem, two rusticated Mr. Brogs that both turn out to hide cracks, and a clean no-name that bears a striking resemblance to a Prince Amlet. As always, the discussion covers condition, shape and staining, smoking appeal, and the question of whether a pipe is worth restoring, with each pipe assigned an ID for the collection catalogue.

## Description

This episode features a mixture of personal smoking pipes and estate acquisitions, ranging from inexpensive budget briars to well-known but still inexpensive Danish makers. The lineup includes a small Monaco pipe purchased during a long work shift, a favored Georg Jensen 22, a Polish-made B&B 040, an unidentified briar marked only with SIX and the word Briar, and a Nørding Compass. The discussion covers smoking characteristics, construction quality, restoration concerns, Danish pipe history, and how personal experience can differ from a pipe's objective quality.

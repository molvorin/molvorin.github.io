## Description

Five estate pipes from two boxes this time, including a massive no-name Danish freehand that might be the biggest pipe in the collection yet. We've got a Le Chevalier Natura 202, a Savinelli 305 Toscana with fantastic graining, a no-name Vittoria bulldog-adjacent piece, a rough no-name bulldog that could make a great restoration project, and that huge unsmoked freehand with a plateau top and high-heel shank. A third box is still waiting to be opened for next time.

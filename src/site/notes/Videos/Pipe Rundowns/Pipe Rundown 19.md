## Description

This episode documents five estate pipes from a variety of makers and backgrounds. The group includes two Savinelli pipes, an English-made Orlik, a Portland-marked pipe, and an unidentified black pipe carrying a pi symbol on the stem. The pipes range from lightly used examples to pieces showing more significant wear, providing a useful snapshot of the collection at this stage.

## Description

Second batch of complete pipes from the Dick lot. Five pipes, mostly in poor condition — a no name billiard with a metal plate, a Baronet 3 Star with a cracked chamber, a Partner Mahogany 401, a Vibel Real Briar, and a rusticated red bent no name. Also notes some accessories found in the box including Dunhill care cloths and a bag of Gaden Piber.

## Description

This episode becomes the unexpected 'video of the twins' as two Mr. Brog 27 pipes and two Mr. Brog 47 pipes appear in the same batch. The collection showcases varying levels of wear, stem damage, cracks, and restoration potential while highlighting how differently identical models can age over time. The final pipe is an unusual vest pocket design that folds for transport and offers an interesting contrast to the larger Polish-made smokers.

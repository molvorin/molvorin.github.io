## Description

This episode features an exceptionally well-restored batch of estate pipes, primarily acquired from a Danish pipe restorer, together with a single French estate pipe added to complete the lineup. The collection includes the Angelo 1382, Lord 53, Absorba 210, Royal Danish 50, and a Jeantet Chambord. Discussion focuses on restoration quality, grain, pipe shapes, smoking comfort, and why this became one of the strongest groups of pipes featured so far.

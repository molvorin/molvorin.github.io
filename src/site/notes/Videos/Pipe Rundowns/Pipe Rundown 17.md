## Description

In Pipe Rundown 17, I take a look at five estate pipes acquired from different sources. The episode begins with two unusual handmade Danish freehand pipes with no maker's marks, both featuring organic carving, pronounced ridges, and decorative 'ears' that make them stand out as display pieces. Next is a Petersons Shamrock 64 from Ireland with attractive grain, a flattened shank, and excellent restored condition. The fourth pipe is a Petersons Year Pipe 2009 Limited Edition, numbered 857 of 1000, which carries both collector appeal and personal significance. The final pipe is a large Lorenzo Egra Filtro 812 fitted for filters and featuring a P-Lip style mouthpiece. As always, each pipe is rated for condition, personal preference, and expected popularity.

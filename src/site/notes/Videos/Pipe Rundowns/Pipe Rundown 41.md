## Description

The second box from Johnny turned out to have eight pipes rather than four - a genuine surprise unpacking. Highlights include two Portland John Peel Specials (one an easy favorite of the whole lot), three Georg Jensens, a pair of Chacom Prestiges, and a Comoy Guildhall with a very faint stamp.

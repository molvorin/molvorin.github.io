## Description

A continuation of the Dick lot, picking up with the complete pipes after the pipe heads and mouthpieces were catalogued in episode 34. Six pipes across a wide range of conditions — an unexpected Stanwell 395 with a crack, a Pimpernel Red churchwarden, a Ben Hur, a Black Prince head moved to the parts box, a no name rusticated bent, and a plain no name briar. Twelve more pipes remain in the box.

## Description

This video examines five estate pipes ranging from a well-known Danish maker to several unidentified examples with unusual shapes and construction. The collection includes a Georg Jensen Orange Extra, two unidentified briar pipes with distinctive forms, an unusual plastic pipe of uncertain purpose, and an English-made VS Straight Grain. Particular attention is given to restoration requirements, stem damage, grain quality, unusual shaping, and the challenge of identifying obscure or heavily worn pipe markings. The selection demonstrates the variety often found in estate pipe lots, where well-established brands appear alongside mysterious pieces that require further research and restoration.

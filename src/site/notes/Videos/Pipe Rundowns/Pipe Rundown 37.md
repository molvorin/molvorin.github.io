## Description

Final batch from the Dick lot, closing out the box. Six pipes, five of them no names — a possible unsmoked hand-cut pipe, a tall furnace-shaped Bruyere Guarantee, a Danish Canadian with a worn mouthpiece, a small lightweight pipe of uncertain material, and a gorilla-taped no name with bird's eye grain and a metal ring. The last pipe is a Stanwell found in its original box, stamped 53 and Made in Denmark, likely a Royal Prince 53.

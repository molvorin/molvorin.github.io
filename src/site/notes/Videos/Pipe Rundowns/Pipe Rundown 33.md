## Description

This is a special episode of Pipe Rundown: rather than a batch of estate pipes, it catalogues the owner's everyday smokers — the four pipes in heaviest daily rotation — plus one extra French pipe brought in to round out the video. The line-up runs from an affordable Cosmos Jupiter and a Danish White Star Carlo to the heavily filled Acire "Spotted Beauty", a beaten-up no-name inherited from a friend, and a chunky no-name French briar (stamped "Vieille Bruyère / Sport Deluxe") with a horn stem and a stubborn loose tenon. Discussion covers the cataloguing and ID system, shape preferences, restoration challenges, the appeal of simple working pipes over showpieces, and the difficulty of getting pipe tobacco in Denmark.

## Description

This episode focuses primarily on pipes from the Poland batch and includes several Mr. Brog examples in different finishes and states of preservation. The selection ranges from near-new examples to heavily used pipes with worn stampings, concluding with a Danish Gloria pipe featuring an unusual stem and distinctive construction. Together they provide an interesting cross-section of practical smoking pipes and restoration candidates.

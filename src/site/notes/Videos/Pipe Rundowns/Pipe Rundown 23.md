## Description

This episode features four estate pipes ranging from a Portland Breuer Guarantee to a compact Dutch-made Big Ben Ranger. The selection includes two unidentified pipes with distinct characteristics and a discussion of a mismatched Stanwell and Georg Jensen combination that was excluded from the catalogue. The group highlights a mixture of elegant shapes, practical smokers, and restoration candidates.

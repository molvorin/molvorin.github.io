## Description

This episode features a diverse selection of pipes ranging from inexpensive modern smokers to distinctive Danish handmade pieces. The lineup includes a Prince Amled pickaxe, two Cosmos Mercury pipes, a striking P. Holtorp wedge, and a Lorenzo Elba complete with original packaging and paperwork. Discussion focuses on shape preferences, Danish pipe makers, collecting habits, restoration considerations, and the difference between appreciating a pipe as an object versus enjoying it as a smoker.

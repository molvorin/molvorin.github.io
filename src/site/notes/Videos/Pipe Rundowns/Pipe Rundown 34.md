## Description

Nine restored estate pipes from a single batch, all acquired from a seller named Per, get their first look and rating in this rundown. Expect a French Jeantet Canadian, a pair of Vauen pipes (including one designed by C. Barrontini for Huber Munchen), a Danish Stanwell Royal Briar, a massive Chap Filtre B-900, a Georg Jensen, an Italian Gasparini, an unmarked Jean-Claude, and a huge Butz-Choquin Corrida to close things out.

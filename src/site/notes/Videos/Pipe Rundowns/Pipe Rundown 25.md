## Description

This episode continues the exploration of the Poland batch and focuses heavily on Mr. Brog pipes, including multiple examples of the 24 shape alongside a damaged 35 and an unidentified model. The selection ranges from heavily used restoration candidates to near-unsmoked examples, allowing for comparisons between older and newer production details. The episode concludes with an unusual Bruyere Garantie pipe featuring an oversized bowl, distinctive engineering, and a shape that stands apart from the rest of the collection.

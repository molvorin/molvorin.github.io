## Description

This episode focuses primarily on Polish-made estate pipes, including three examples from Mr. Brog and a B&B 019, alongside a Danish Prince Amhed. The pipes range from near-unsmoked examples to restoration candidates with damaged stems and structural issues. Discussion covers Vinted purchases, replacement mouthpieces, condition assessments, and the surprising appeal of inexpensive working pipes compared to more traditional Danish briars.

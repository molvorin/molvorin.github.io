## Description

This episode of Pipe Rundown catalogues five more pipes from the collection, an unusually varied lot. It opens with an elegant no-name that the owner has long enjoyed smoking despite never identifying it, followed by a slim BBB Classic 652 billiard made in England. The back half turns Swedish, with two Brillon system pipes: a plastic-bodied Brillon 60 and a full Brillon Populär set with interchangeable briar chamber heads. The episode closes with a Mr. Brog 41, one of the owner's favourite shapes. Discussion covers shape preferences, the Brillon and Falcon-style interchangeable system, Danish and Swedish pipe makers, restoration considerations, and the contrast between affordable working pipes and more refined examples.

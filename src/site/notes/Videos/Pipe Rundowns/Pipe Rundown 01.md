## Description
A introductory episode from a new pipe restoration channel. I am based in Denmark, and go through a first batch of 10 estate pipes, rating each on condition (0–9), personal preference/favorite (0–9), and estimated value (0–9). I also show my first, and partially successful restoration — a cracked pipe that i glued and buffed — reflecting on lessons learned (sandpaper grit, glue viscosity, mouthpiece alignment).

Second episode of the pipe sorting series. I go through another 10 estate pipes, rating each on condition, personal preference, and estimated popularity/value. I mention having adjusted the audio settings after watching back the first video, and note that better lighting is on the way. I also share a piece of research from since the last video: BBB stands for "Britain's Best Briar" — and before the 40s it referenced the original owner's name (Blumfeld's Best Briars). Plans remain the same: sort all pipes first, then tackle cleaning and restoration in dedicated videos, with links to be added in descriptions over time.

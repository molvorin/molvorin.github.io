## Description
This video explores five estate pipes with a wide variety of shapes, histories, and restoration stories. The collection includes examples from makers such as Kriswill, Havana, Graco, and BPK, alongside an unidentified Pipe Makers model. Some pipes show evidence of previous repairs and restoration work, while others remain largely original and require only routine maintenance. Particular attention is given to unusual chamber proportions, grain patterns, stem designs, and the craftsmanship that gives each pipe its individual character. The video also highlights how even heavily used or repaired pipes can retain their appeal through thoughtful design and comfortable handling.

## Description

The longest unboxing yet: a mystery box that turned out to hold nine pipes plus a pile of accessories, including a surprise Peterson's Aran, a hefty Savinelli Extra 125, and a leather-wrapped Kiko 341 from Tanganyika. There are also matching Edinhall Danish Design twins, a massive Mayfair bulldog, a two-piece 'Frankenstein' no-name, and a Barry bulldog. Two pieces, an unsmoked NB8 with a cracked mortise and a broken meerschaum-stemmed piece, didn't make the cut as complete pipes. All for 400 kroner including a full set of pipe tools, tampers, a Zippo, and a Teak Denmark pipe stand.

## Description

This episode focuses on pipes from the Poland batch and includes three Mr. Brog pipes, a B&B 022, and a second example of the Mr. Brog 27 shape in an alternative finish. The group offers an opportunity to compare similar shapes, construction methods, and condition levels within a single acquisition batch.

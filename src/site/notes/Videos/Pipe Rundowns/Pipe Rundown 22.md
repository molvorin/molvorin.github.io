## Description

This episode presents a varied group of pipes including a Missouri Meerschaum corn cob pipe, a possible Comoy Guildhall, a Mogul Extra, a Stanwell Selected Briar, and a Dollar Sweden system pipe. The collection spans several countries, materials, and construction methods, ranging from simple utilitarian designs to more distinctive factory-made and branded examples.

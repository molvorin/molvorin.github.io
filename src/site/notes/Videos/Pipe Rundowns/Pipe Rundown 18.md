## Description

I examine five estate pipes ranging from heavily damaged restoration candidates to well-preserved Italian briars. The episode begins with a large Don briar suffering from severe stem damage, making it an ideal candidate for practicing stem repair techniques. Next is an unsmoked Polish-made B&B 020, a lightweight pipe fitted with a stinger and preserved in near-new condition. The third pipe is an unidentified briar marked with references to Bruyere and a coarse finish, featuring a loose stem but otherwise solid condition. The final two pipes are Italian Savinelli examples: a Brupa 603 and a Padova 173 bulldog, both attractive smokers in excellent estate condition. Throughout the video, each pipe is evaluated for condition, personal preference, and expected popularity.

## Description

Unboxing of a cheap lot purchased from a seller called Dick. The box turns out to contain far more than expected — a large bag of loose mouthpieces (none stamped), a wooden cigar holder, a pipe tool, a 15mm rimmer, a Føljesven multi-tool, Dollar pipe filters, and a porcelain pipe with a hunting motif and a huge chamber.

The bulk of the video goes through around 18 pipe heads found loose in the box — including a Pimpernel Red, a Portland Briar Guarantee, a Mayfair London 539, a Guildhall London, a Georg Jensen handmade, a BBB Classic, a meerschaum with no stamps, a Garde Sana Freestyle, and several unnamed heads in various states of damage. Most have broken tenons, cracks, or missing mouthpieces. They are collected into a pipe head box for future sanding and polishing practice.

The video ends when complete pipes begin to appear — those are covered in episodes 35, 36 and 37.

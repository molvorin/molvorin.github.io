## Description
Third episode of the pipe sorting series. Another 10 pipes reviewed using the same rating system: condition, personal preference, and estimated popularity/value. I introduce a new addition to the coding system: a video number prefix (3 for this episode) to make it easier to link back to specific pipes when restoration videos are made later. I also mention ongoing improvements to video and audio quality, with better lighting on the way. A recurring observation is that many pipes from the L lot share the same brand names, suggesting they may have come from a single collector who favoured certain brands.

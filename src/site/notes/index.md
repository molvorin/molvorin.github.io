# MOLVORIN

This is a site that catalogs my smoking pipes. I have a channel on Youtube and Peertube, where I initially look through all the estate pipes that I have bought, and rate them based on my uninformed gut feeling. On this site, I try to sort them and create an overview of what we've gone through in the videos.

I use a note taking program called Obsidian, that has an option to publish online, and I thought it could be interesting for some of you to follow along on some of my notes. Some areas of particular interest, could be:

- [[Overviews/All Pipe IDs\|All Pipe IDs]] - Here you will find a list of all the pipes, and links to the individual pipe, along with the unique ID number that basically consists of information on the pipes condition, how much I like them, estimated popularity as well as what video they feature in, what number pipe they are in that vide and any potential batch that I got them from.
- [[Overviews/CPP\|CPP]] - A list of all pipes, with sortable attributes for condition, preference and (estimated) popularity
-  [[Overviews/Manufacturet overview\|Manufacturet overview]] - A list of all the pipes, grouped into manufacturer groups. This is often a guessed manufacturer, based on what I can see on the actual pipe. Don't take it at face value
-  [[Overviews/Video overview\|Video overview]] - All the pipes grouped under videos that they feature in

## About

I started pipe smoking late in 2025 and have been gradually become better at it. It is a long process for me, and I expect it to take some time. That said, what has gone a lot faster, is growing a steady collection of estate pipes in questionable states.

I live in Denmark, that to my surprise, seems to have a massive heritage within the smoking pipe history. This also allows me to often find good pipes for low prices, although many of them have been heavily used and some even abused.

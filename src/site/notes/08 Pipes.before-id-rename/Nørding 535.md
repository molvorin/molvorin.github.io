## Description

A well-preserved Danish-made Nørding pipe in excellent smoking condition. The bowl shows very little wear, with almost no cake buildup and only minor interior cleaning required. The pipe appears to have undergone restoration or exceptionally careful maintenance at some point in its life, preserving much of its original appearance. Its classic Danish shaping, comfortable proportions, and clean condition make it a practical and attractive smoker, while subtle details in the stem and stamping hint at an older production period.

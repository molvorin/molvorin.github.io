## Description

This is a classic Falcon system pipe and one of the first pipes I ever bought online after hearing so much about Falcon pipes. Even though I personally am not a huge fan of how it looks or smokes, I can absolutely appreciate why people enjoy them. It accepts filters, is easy to clean, and remains in very good condition overall. The pipe does not really need restoration work and can simply go back onto the shelf. I also talk a bit about the interchangeable bowl system and my curiosity about some of the pipe’s design details and accessories.

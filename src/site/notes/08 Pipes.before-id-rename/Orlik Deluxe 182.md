## Description

A substantial English-made Orlik pipe with a large tobacco chamber and a lightly rusticated finish. The bowl remains in very good condition and requires little more than routine cleaning, although the stem shows signs of wear that would benefit from polishing and maintenance. The pipe has a long, traditional profile and solid proportions that suit smokers who prefer extended smoking sessions. While the rustication adds texture and character, the oversized bowl is the most defining feature, offering significant capacity without compromising structural integrity

## Description

This first pipe is a worn but attractive Danish-made Georg Jensen briar with beautiful grain patterns and birdseye. Structurally, it seems fairly sound with no visible bowl cracks, though the stem is heavily worn, discolored, and likely not original because of its unusually long shape. I suspect this may become one of my future restoration projects because the briar itself still has a lot of character. The markings appear to read “Georg Jensen Made in Denmark,” “Dania,” and either “606” or “909.” While the condition is only average overall, I really appreciate the grain and think it has potential.

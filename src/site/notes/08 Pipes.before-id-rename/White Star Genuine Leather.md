## Description

This pipe is a unique White Star leather-covered pipe with what appears to be a clay-lined bowl interior. It clearly shows signs of heavy use, including burn wear and accumulated dirt, but overall it still feels structurally decent. The leather stitching appears intact, and I find the pipe visually interesting despite not knowing the proper way to clean leather-covered pipes yet. The markings include “White Star,” “Genuine Leather,” and the shape number “623.” This could become a very interesting pipe if I can clean and restore it properly

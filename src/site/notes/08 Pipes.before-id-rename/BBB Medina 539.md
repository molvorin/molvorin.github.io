## Description

A tall and elegant BBB Medina pipe featuring a distinctive flattened bowl profile rather than a traditional rounded shape. The briar displays attractive grain patterns that become more apparent under proper lighting, contributing to a refined and sophisticated appearance. The pipe appears to have been restored previously and remains in very good condition, showing only minor cake inside the chamber. Its unusual proportions, clean lines, and balanced form create a pipe that feels both traditional and slightly unconventional at the same time.
